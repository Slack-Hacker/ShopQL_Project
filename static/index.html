ShopQL Formal Grammar & Syntax Reference

            
ShopQL is an SQL-inspired Domain-Specific Language designed for e-commerce search, filtering, sorting, and inventory transactions.

            
            
1. Search & Filter (FIND / SHOW)

            
FIND PRODUCTS [WHERE <condition>] [ORDER BY <column> [ASC|DESC]] [LIMIT <n>];
SHOW PRODUCTS [WHERE <condition>] [ORDER BY <column> [ASC|DESC]] [LIMIT <n>];

            
            
2. Inventory Management (ADD, RESTOCK, BUY)

            
ADD PRODUCT "<name>" PRICE <num> [CATEGORY "<cat>"] [STOCK <n>] [RATING <num>];
RESTOCK PRODUCT "<name>" QUANTITY <n>;
BUY PRODUCT "<name>" QUANTITY <n>;


            
3. Update & Delete

            
UPDATE PRODUCT "<name>" SET PRICE = <val>, STOCK = <val>;
DELETE PRODUCT "<name>";
DELETE PRODUCTS WHERE STOCK <= 0;
